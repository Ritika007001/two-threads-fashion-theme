# Installing the Two Threads theme

A step-by-step guide for beginners. You need a Shopify store; a free trial store works.

## 1. Get the theme zip

You need a file called something like `Two Threads-1.0.0.zip`.

- If someone sent you the zip, you're ready. Don't unzip it.
- If you only have the code from GitHub, open the repository, click the green **Code** button, then **Download ZIP**. Unzip it, open the folder inside, select the eight theme folders (`assets`, `blocks`, `config`, `layout`, `locales`, `sections`, `snippets`, `templates`) plus `LICENSE` and the `licenses` folder, and zip **those items**, not the folder around them. Shopify needs the folders at the top level of the zip.

## 2. Upload it to Shopify

1. In Shopify admin, go to **Online Store > Themes**.
2. Scroll to **Theme library** and click **Add theme**, then **Upload zip file**.
3. Choose the zip and click **Upload**.

The theme appears in your Theme library. Uploading does **not** change what your shoppers see. Your current theme stays live until you click **Publish**.

## 3. Have a look around

In the Theme library, click the **...** button next to Two Threads, then **Preview**. The home page already looks complete: it ships with a built-in banner photo, four category tiles, four sample products, an "Our story" photo and three sample journal stories. All of these are stand-ins for your own content.

To start editing, click **Customize**. Everything below happens in that editor. Click **Save** in the top right after each change.

## 4. What to customize first

Work through these in order.

**Store name and logo**

- Click the **Header** at the top of the page. Type your store name in **Logo text**. The theme shows it in the heading font.

**Colours**

- Click the paintbrush icon (**Theme settings**) in the left bar, then **Colors**.
- Pick one of the four ready-made schemes: **Cream** (warm, the default), **Sage** (soft green), **Ivory** (brightest) or **Charcoal** (dark). The whole store changes at once.
- To fine-tune, click a scheme and adjust its five colours. Keep text dark on a light background, or light on a dark one, so it stays easy to read.
- Each section also has a **Background** choice: page colour or the alternate colour, for a bit of rhythm down the page.

**Images**

Every built-in photo is replaced the moment you choose your own.

- **Banner:** click the **Hero banner** section, then **Select image**. Use a wide photo with empty space on the left for the heading (about 2400 by 1340 pixels). If your photo is dark, switch **Text colour over the image** to the background colour.
- **Category tiles:** click **Shop by category**, then each tile. Choose the collection it links to and, optionally, an image. If you don't pick an image, the tile uses the collection's own image, then the built-in one.
- **Our story:** click **Image with text** and choose a portrait photo (4:5, about 1600 by 2000 pixels).
- **Product photos** live with your products in **Products** in Shopify admin, not in the theme. Portrait 4:5 photos look best; the theme crops everything to that shape.

**Menus**

- **Header:** click the header, then **Add block > Menu link** for each page or collection you want in the top menu. Give each one a label and a link.
- **Footer:** click the footer. Add **Link column** blocks for your pages, a **Newsletter** block and a **Store details** block with your address and opening hours.

**Text**

- Click any heading, button or paragraph to edit it. Each home page section also has an **Eyebrow label**, the small capitals above the heading such as NEW ARRIVALS. Leave it empty to hide it.
- Wording that isn't in a section, such as "Add to cart", is under **Online Store > Themes > ... > Edit default theme content**.

## 5. Before you launch: pick a collection in Featured pieces

This is the important one. Until you choose a collection, the **Featured pieces** section shows **four sample products** with built-in photos and made-up prices. They are not real products. Shoppers can see them, and clicking one goes to your "All products" page.

1. Click the **Featured pieces** section on the home page.
2. Under **Collection**, click **Select collection** and pick one of yours.
3. Click **Save**.

The samples disappear and your real products take their place.

The same idea applies elsewhere, but these sort themselves out as you add content:

- **From the journal** shows three sample stories until your blog has posts.
- **Shop by category** tiles show built-in photos until you pick an image or the collection has one.
- The product page shows **sample reviews, a sample rating and example size charts**. Replace them with your own, or remove those blocks.

## 6. Launch checklist

- [ ] Store name set in the header
- [ ] A colour scheme chosen
- [ ] Banner and "Our story" photos replaced
- [ ] A collection picked in Featured pieces
- [ ] Category tiles linked to real collections
- [ ] Header and footer menus filled in
- [ ] Sample reviews, ratings and size chart measurements replaced
- [ ] A test order placed, from product page to checkout

When everything looks right, go to **Online Store > Themes**, click the **...** button next to Two Threads, then **Publish**.
